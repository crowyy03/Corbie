## Description
Corbie is a shared space for two. Today, plans, dates, wishes and lists, without the chat noise.
One place you both see, so nobody has to ask a third time.
What matters lands on the home screen, in a widget, without opening the app.

TODAY
The day on one screen. Your plans in a row you can swipe, the tasks due today, what starts today, and the tasks nobody took yet. Tick one without leaving the screen.

TASKS
A to-do list for two. Anyone can take a task, or hand it back. Nobody is the boss here. Due dates, repeats, and a note for the phone number you always lose.

CALENDAR
The dates you both keep. Birthdays, trips, the dentist. Anniversaries and days together show up on their own. Import once from the iPhone calendar, keep the rest here.

WHEN YOU ARE BOTH FREE
Corbie reads both calendars and shows the windows you have in common. It shares when you are busy, never what you are doing. Titles, places and guests stay on your phone.

WISHES
Drop a link. Name, price and photo show up on their own. Share from any app straight into Corbie. Your partner never sees that you were looking.

PLANS
A trip, a kitchen, a wedding, a car. Set the amount, add money in any currency, see what is left. One pot, no "who owes whom". Each plan keeps its own prep list, and a step with a date turns up in Today.

LISTS
Places to go, things to watch, what to cook. One adds, the other ticks. A list with places gets a map. The shopping list has its own widget with real checkboxes.

CAPSULES AND VOTES
Write a letter that opens in a year. Nobody opens it early, not you, not them. Ask a question in secret and the answer stays hidden until you have both answered.

PEOPLE
The birthdays that matter and the gift ideas you saved. Two weeks before the date Corbie says something, once, and tells you whether anything is picked.

SUNDAY RECAP
On Sunday evening Corbie counts the week: what each of you closed, how the plans moved, what is coming. It costs nothing and stays free after the trial.

WIDGETS
Thirteen of them. Days together, the next date, three tasks you tick from the home screen, the shopping list, a plan and how far you got. Lock screen too.

ONE SUBSCRIPTION
One plan covers both of you. Seven days free, then 4.99 a month or 29.99 a year. Inviting your partner is always free. When the trial ends the calendar, Today and the weekly recap keep working.

PRIVACY
No ads. No third-party SDKs. Your data lives in your iCloud, not on our servers.
